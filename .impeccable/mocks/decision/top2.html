Kriken Låtgissning: två riktningar för lagens skärm

Static mockups at a real phone size (390 × 844). The content, buttons and flow are the ones in today's game; only the look is different. Each direction is shown in two states: your turn while you place a card, and following another team's turn.

A · Festivalbiljetten

Every song is a ticket stub with the year as the big number; every team has a woven wristband in its colour. Tap the perforation between two stubs and the strip opens for the new card. All teams are visible at once, with no sideways scrolling.

B · Jukeboxen

Every song is a jukebox title strip: the year in the selection-code box and the artist in the printed band, all in the team's colour. Teams are chunky selector buttons, and "Lås" is the red jukebox button.

Team names and album-art colours are made-up sample content; in the game, the real team names and Spotify album covers appear.
